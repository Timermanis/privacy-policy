# Privacy Policy for Supplements TrackMyStack

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026

**Supplements TrackMyStack** ("the App") is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
* **Local Data Storage:** The App stores all your supplement logs, schedules, stack configurations, and app preferences locally on your device using an embedded database (`SQLite`).
* **No Server Transmission:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or process any of your personal data, supplement logs, or usage metrics on external servers or cloud services.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the App.

## 2. Third-Party Services
* The App does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, analytics tracking, or remote data collection services.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
* Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data.
* You can clear or delete your data at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

## 4. Permissions
* The App requires standard local permissions necessary for core operation (such as local storage access and local notifications for supplement reminders, if enabled).

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
* **Email:** support@janistimermanis.com (or your preferred developer contact email)
